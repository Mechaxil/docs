## Overview
The D4184 Mosfet Opto Module is an inexpensive prototype module used for safer switching of higher current and voltage outputs. It features an optocoupler for isolation and a MOSFET for switching, making it ideal for controlling motors, LEDs, or other high-power devices from microcontrollers or other low-voltage control signals.
