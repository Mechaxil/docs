  ## Wiring

  The Pico I/O Board is a **pass-through breakout**: each Pico GPIO appears on labeled headers.

 <img src="/img/products/pico-io-board/Pico-IO-Board-illustration.drawio.png" alt="Pico I/O Board pinout diagram" />

  The board doesn't contain any active components, level shifting, or protection circuitry.  
  It's designed to be simple and easy to use, but be mindful of the electrical characteristics of your Pico and peripherals.

</div>

<div className="mx-section-card">
  ## Electrical Specifications

  - Voltage: 3.3V logic levels (or 5V if using the 5V pins, but be careful with 5V peripherals, as the Pico's GPIO is not 5V tolerant)
  - Current: Each pin can source/sink up to 12mA, with a total maximum of 50mA across all pins.
  - Protection: No built-in protection, so use external components as needed for your application.
  - GPIO pin to pico-io-board last pin resistance is approximately 0.06Ω.

</div>
